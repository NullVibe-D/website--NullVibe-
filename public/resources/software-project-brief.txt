SOFTWARE PROJECT BRIEF | NullVibe
Copy this outline. One or two sentences per field is enough to start.

1. Problem: Who is affected, what do they do today, and what goes wrong?

2. Outcome: What should improve? What baseline can we record now?

3. Workflow: Who starts the task? Who approves it? What happens on failure?

4. First release: What is the smallest useful journey a user must finish?

5. Out of scope: Which useful features can wait?

6. Data: Which tools, records, permissions or offline needs matter?

7. Acceptance: Describe one normal example and one edge case.
   What outcome should each produce?

8. Delivery: Budget range, deadline and reason, decision maker,
   maintenance owner and unresolved questions.
